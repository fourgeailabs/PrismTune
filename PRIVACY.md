# PrismTune Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

PrismTune is a music player for your own Plex media server, developed by FourgeAI LABS. This policy explains what PrismTune does and does not do with your information.

## What PrismTune is

PrismTune plays music from a Plex server that you own or have access to. You need your own Plex account and Plex server to use it. PrismTune is unofficial and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Plex Inc. Plex's own privacy policy governs your Plex account and your Plex server.

## Information stored on your device only

PrismTune stores the following on your device only, and does not send it to FourgeAI LABS:

- Your Plex sign-in token, stored in encrypted storage on your device, so you stay signed in. Your Plex password is never stored, never logged, and is used only once to obtain that token.
- Your selected Plex server and connection details, so the app can reconnect to your server.
- Your playback history inside PrismTune, including your most recently played song, your queue and playback position, and your most-played tracks, so the app can resume playback, show recently played music, and build the Offline Top Picks playlist.
- Music and artwork downloaded for offline playback, stored in the app's private storage on your device.
- App settings, including your safe-area preference, shuffle and repeat settings, and diagnostics history.

Signing out clears your stored Plex token. Uninstalling the app deletes the data PrismTune stored on your device.

## Information sent to Plex and your server

To function, PrismTune must send requests to Plex and to your Plex server, including:

- Your Plex sign-in token, to authenticate with Plex and your server.
- Requests for your music library, artists, albums, tracks, playlists, and artwork, and requests to stream or download tracks you choose to play.

That information goes to Plex and to your server, not to FourgeAI LABS. PrismTune does not sell, share, or otherwise disclose your information to any other party.

## What PrismTune does not do

PrismTune does not contain ads, does not use analytics or tracking, does not create an account with FourgeAI LABS, does not collect your email address, contacts, location, or device identifiers for FourgeAI LABS, and does not sell your data.

## Children

PrismTune is a general music player and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be posted here with a new effective date.
